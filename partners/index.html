How a pilot works

Small, written down, and honest.

We prefer a short pilot with a clear question over a big launch with a vague one.

  1. A first conversation. Who it is for, what you already offer, what worries you.
  2. Scope in writing. Who takes part, for how long, what is measured, and what data is shared with whom.
  3. The pilot. Your group uses Antara on their own phones, with our team on hand.
  4. A straight review. What helped, what did not, and whether to continue.

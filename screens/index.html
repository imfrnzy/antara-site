Patterns

  • Faint and dotted after one mention. Firm after two. She does not build a story off a single sentence.
  • Heavy topics wait. Grief and the like only appear after two separate talks.
  • No fake milestones. An empty sky shows soft ghost stars that say what would light them, never a blank screen.
  • You answer, not her. A small question comes days later: is it lighter, the same, or heavier? Only your tap counts. Silence is never marked as resolved.
